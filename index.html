How to play

Survive endless bot waves. Every kill pays out cash for weapons, attachments, skins and gear. Every kill also levels up the gun you used, for good. Press B any time in a run to shop.

Movement tricks

Jump while sliding to keep your speed. Jump during a wall-run to kick off the wall. Hold Space into a ledge up to about 2.3m high to climb onto it, or to vault a low wall. Charged jump: crouch, hold Space (a bar fills under the crosshair), then let go to launch up to about 3x as high.

The climb

Elevator doors. After each floor you pick one of three doors (keys 1, 2, 3). Each one changes the rules for the next floor, like low gravity, one-hit kills or a swarm of weak bots. Riskier doors pay more cash.
Titan. Every 10th floor ends with a giant boss. Shoot the glowing core for triple damage. When it stomps, jump: being in the air dodges the shockwave.
Paint meter. Kills fill the paint meter at the top. When it's full you get a short frenzy with 1.5× damage.
Spell mutations. Some mutations are spells that cast themselves: Fireball and Thunderstrike fire off your hits, Meteor Call and Frost Nova go off on kills, and Black Hole opens on headshot kills. They show up in the normal mutation picks with a SPELL tag.
Map sizes. Every floor rolls a size. Most are small: tiny arenas and small maps have fewer bots, but they are tougher, more often elite, and harder types show up sooner. Sometimes you get a big map or a huge open desert where you want a rifle.
Night waves. From floor 2, about 1 wave in 5 goes pitch black. Without a light you really can't see. A Tac Light on your gun (under-barrel attachment, $250) lights a beam in front of you and stays on while you sprint. Night Vision Goggles ($5,000, Gear tab) let you see the whole map in green for the rest of the run: press L to switch them on or off. Day comes back when the wave is cleared.
Player level and daily quests. Everything you do earns XP. Each level makes the start of every run easier. Three new quests every day, and a streak bonus for coming back.
Levels, not power. Recon Scan and movement abilities start weak and cheap. Upgrade them in the buy menu (Support and Abilities tabs) to make them strong.
Character upgrades. Every player level gives 1 upgrade point. Spend points in Character on max health (up to 200 HP) or speed (up to +30%). You can refund them any time.
Daily Tower. One seeded run per day, the same maps and doors for everyone, on Normal. Your best floor for the day is saved.

Controller

Left stick moves, right stick looks. RT fires, LT aims. A jumps, B crouches, X reloads, Y switches weapon. RB dashes, LB throws a frag. Click the left stick to sprint. D-pad: up grapple, down mutation, left recon scan, right night vision. Crouch with B, then hold A to charge a big jump. Hold R3 for the detailed inspect. Menu pauses, View opens the shop. Change controller look speed in Settings.

Know your enemy

Bots start unaware. They have to see or hear you: "?" means suspicious, "!" means spotted.

They learn

Bots adapt to you. Their skill (top of the screen) climbs all the time, and faster when you shoot accurately and land headshots. Higher tiers react faster, lead your movement, aim for your head, A-D strafe and crouch-peek. At Sweat they play like the best players you've met. Every third floor is a Rampage: weak bots, bottomless ammo, go wild.

Evolve your guns

Kills with a gun earn mutation picks (press N). Each mutation has three levels and starts mild. Upgrade your starting Pip-9 in the Sidearm Workshop tab of the buy menu, all the way to Pip Prime.
