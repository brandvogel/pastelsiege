Privacy Policy

Last updated 28 November 2026

Pastel Siege ("the game", "we", "us") is a browser game at pastelsiege.com. This policy explains what information the game collects, why, who it is shared with, and the choices you have. We collect as little as we can. There are no ads, and we don't sell your information.

Playing without an account

You can play without an account. Your progress, settings and unlocks are saved only in your own browser (local storage) and never sent to us. Clearing your browser data deletes them.

What we collect when you make an account

  • Email address, to sign you in, confirm your account, and send sign-in or password reset links. We only email you about your account; there's no newsletter or marketing email.
  • Username, which other players can see (for example on leaderboards, friend lists and in co-op).
  • Password, if you choose one. It is stored only as a secure hash by our account provider; we can't see it.
  • Google or Discord sign-in, if you use it: we receive your email address, and your account provider may also pass on your name and profile picture. We use the email address to sign you in. We don't use the name or picture, and the game never shows them to other players.
  • Game data: your cloud save (progress, unlocks, settings), scores you submit to leaderboards, friends and friend requests, your role (such as tester or moderator), and moderation records such as kicks and mutes.
  • Online status: while you play signed in, the game records that you're online and when you were last seen, so friends can see it.

Multiplayer

Co-op games connect players directly (peer to peer) where possible. That means the players in your game can see your IP address, as with most online games. If a direct connection isn't possible, messages go through our relay server instead. Chat messages are sent to the players in your game and aren't stored by us. The PeerJS public server is used to help players find each other.

Information collected automatically

Like any website, the services that host the game receive technical information when you visit, such as your IP address, browser type and the time of the request. They use it to deliver the game and keep it secure. The game itself has no analytics, tracking cookies or advertising.

Who we share it with

We only share information with the services that run the game, and only for that purpose:

  • Supabase: accounts, sign-in, cloud saves and leaderboards.
  • Vercel: hosts the website.
  • Cloudflare: domain name service, and the connection to our multiplayer relay.
  • Google and Discord: only if you choose to sign in with them.
  • Our email provider, to send account emails.
  • jsDelivr: delivers code libraries the game uses.
  • PeerJS: helps co-op players connect.

Some of these services store data outside Australia (for example, in the United States). We will also share information if the law requires it.

How long we keep it

We keep your account information until you delete your account. Unconfirmed accounts and moderation records may be removed after a while. Copies in backups are deleted on their normal schedule.

Your choices and rights

  • You can change your email address in the game.
  • You can ask us for a copy of your information, ask us to correct it, or ask us to delete your account and all of its data. Email hello@pastelsiege.com from the address on your account (or tell us your username), and we'll do it within 30 days.
  • You can stop using Google or Discord sign-in at any time from your Google or Discord account settings.

Children

The game is not directed at children under 13. If you are under 13, ask a parent or guardian before making an account. If you are a parent and think your child has given us information without your permission, email us and we will delete it.

Security

All connections to the game are encrypted (HTTPS). Access to account data is restricted so that players can only read and change their own information. No system is perfectly secure, so please use a password you don't use anywhere else.

Changes to this policy

If we change this policy, we'll update the date at the top. For important changes, we'll also let players know in the game.

Contact

Questions or requests: hello@pastelsiege.com. If you're not happy with our answer, you can complain to the Office of the Australian Information Commissioner (oaic.gov.au).
